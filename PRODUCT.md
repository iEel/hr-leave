# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Employees, managers, HR staff, and system administrators at Sonic Interfreight, Grandlink Logistics, and Sonic Autologis. They use the system during routine work to request leave, approve team leave, manage employee records, review balances, and check attendance data.

## Product Purpose

HR Leave Management centralizes leave requests, approvals, leave balances, employee administration, attendance visibility, reporting, and audit trails. Success means users can complete everyday HR tasks quickly, with clear status, low confusion, and reliable Thai-language workflows.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, practical. The product should feel like a trusted internal operations tool: clear, steady, and efficient rather than promotional or decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not make the interface feel like a marketing landing page, a decorative analytics demo, or a card-heavy layout where every small fact gets oversized treatment. Avoid excessive whitespace, loud gradients, ornamental motion, and UI that distracts from repeated operational tasks.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize task speed: common employee and HR workflows should be immediately visible and easy to scan.
- Keep density appropriate: dashboard information should be compact but readable, especially for daily repeated use.
- Make status obvious: approvals, balances, attendance, and errors should communicate state without extra explanation.
- Preserve familiar patterns: use standard navigation, tables, buttons, forms, and cards consistently across the product.
- Design Thai-first: labels, spacing, and responsive behavior must support Thai text cleanly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for readable contrast, keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus states, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion friendly interactions. The UI should remain usable in both light and dark mode and on desktop or mobile screens.
